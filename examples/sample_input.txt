Geoffrey Hinton and Yann LeCun are pioneers of deep learning. Hinton developed backpropagation techniques at the University of Toronto, and LeCun created convolutional neural networks at Bell Labs. In 2018, Hinton, LeCun and Yoshua Bengio received the Turing Award for their work. Bengio founded Mila, a research institute in Montreal. Today LeCun works at Meta, where he leads fundamental AI research, while Hinton left Google in 2023 to speak openly about the risks of artificial intelligence.
